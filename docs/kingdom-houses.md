## What this is (and is not)

Kingdom houses are a **chain of reasoning** — mandatory stages for making sense of a request before action. They are **not**:

- Bishop `primary_role` values (the fourteen registry roles)
- Interaction classes
- Permission or Boss Moves
- A composite soul score
- A fixed deployment of twelve always-on agents

Kingdom output is **counsel**. Policy authorizes. Witnesses prove. House 7 synthesis cannot turn invalid intent into authorized action by majority vote.

## The 7 Houses (pipeline)

| # | House id | Stage | Paired counsel |
|---|----------|-------|----------------|
| 1 | `house:intent` | Intent | Clarity / Ambiguity |
| 2 | `house:valid-intent` | Valid Intent | Intent / Execution |
| 3 | `house:concept` | Concept | Structure / Flow |
| 4 | `house:notion` | Notion | Risk / Opportunity |
| 5 | `house:solvency` | Solvency | **Precision / Vision** → [detail](./solvency-house.md) |
| 6 | `house:micro-slice` | Micro-Slice | Logic / Intuition |
| 7 | `house:action` | Action | **Authorized synthesis runtime** (Empire / operator+policy bounds) |

Houses 1–6 are owned by the six contrasting pairs. House 7 is synthesis and action under real authority — not a seventh ballot pair.

Legacy label “Empire” means synthesis owner (historically Nephew-facing). It never overrides actual operator, policy, permission, or platform authority.

## The 6 contrasting pairs (12 Kingdom sides)

King / Queen are **legacy lens labels** for the two sides of a pair — not gender, not protected-trait inference, not rank.

| Pair | Side A (legacy “King”) | Side B (legacy “Queen”) | House |
|------|------------------------|-------------------------|-------|
| Clarity vs Ambiguity | Clarity — explicit, source-grounded meaning | Ambiguity — uncertainty, alternatives, missing decisions | Intent |
| Intent vs Execution | Intent — desired outcome, purpose, bounds | Execution — concrete verifiable path | Valid Intent |
| Structure vs Flow | Structure — hierarchy, placement, interfaces | Flow — sequence, continuity, usability | Concept |
| Risk vs Opportunity | Risk — failure, harm, cost, downside | Opportunity — benefit, leverage, reuse, upside | Notion |
| Precision vs Vision | Precision — exactness, completeness, measurement | Vision — direction, durability, scale, long-term fit | Solvency |
| Logic vs Intuition | Logic — explicit dependencies, consistency | Intuition — patterns/hypotheses for evidence-backed review | Micro-Slice |

Both sides of a **selected** pair must produce ballots (or the pair is marked incomplete). One side cannot silently impersonate the pair.

## Routing modes

| Mode | Behavior |
|------|----------|
| `targeted` | Only pairs relevant to the task; record why others were skipped |
| `full-court` | All six pairs — only when explicitly requested or policy requires |

Full-court is never inferred merely because twelve sides exist. Routine reversible tasks should not spin twelve synthetic voices.

## Ballot contract (counsel artifact)

A ballot carries: stable IDs (ballot, route, pair, Kingdom, House, source), claims, evidence references, assumptions, alternatives, recommendation, confidence, limitations, author + independent review identities, timestamps, content hash.

Must **not** store private hidden chain-of-thought. Allowed artifacts: claims, evidence refs, assumptions, alternatives, recommendations, confidence, limitations.

## Hierarchy inside each Kingdom side

```text
Workers (default ×3, policy-controlled)
  → Supervisor
  → Manager
  → Director (King/Queen lens side)
  → House 7 synthesis (authorized runtime under operator + policy)
```

Hard rules:

- Separation of duties
- Self-approval forbidden — ballot author cannot be sole reviewer
- Reviewer cannot invent missing source evidence
- Counsel cannot approve its own external side effect
- Synthesis cannot erase dissent, missing ballots, or limitations

Mandatory stack at every level (doctrine):

1. Automata Belief System  
2. Valid Intent Questions  
3. Microslice Solvency Formula  
4. Chain of Reasoning through the assigned House
