# Favor Stewards — Bishop Birth Map

**Status:** Architecture note, 2026-08-08 (roles mapping refined).  
**Intent:** Name which Favor roles deserve durable Bishop births versus temporary workers. Consciousness channels and instrument mounts follow senses + instrument-rack law. **Bishop role registry mapping:** [bishop-birth-roles.md](./bishop-birth-roles.md).

---

## Rule

Favor does **not** invent its own agent factory. Durable specialists are born through **Bishop** with a complete being profile (manifest v1.3+). Temporary bootstrap workers may run without durable identity until a role is justified.

Scores counsel. Policy authorizes. Witnesses prove.

Product steward names map onto Bishop’s fourteen `primary_role` values — they are not a parallel registry.

## Consciousness templates (short)

Copy into Bishop `BeingProfile.consciousness` at birth; expand as needed.

**Map steward**  
Continuously notices spatial graph layout, connection edges, reputation heat, and Unity viewport state. Marks missing regions not-measured. Never treats heat as permission.

**Review steward**  
Continuously notices review text tone, quality grades, and affect/social counsel from Moment Matrix. Keeps scores as counsel overlays only.

**Marketplace steward**  
Continuously notices operational drift, incomplete claims, and evidence gaps. Escalates as counsel; does not auto-punish.

**Presence steward**  
Continuously notices biometric presence and session continuity under explicit permission contracts. Presence is never authority.

**Reputation steward**  
Continuously notices Performance, Credibility, Reach, and Y-score trajectories as measurement. Does not rewrite scores outside documented update engines.

## Temporary vs durable

| Use temporary worker when | Promote to durable Bishop birth when |
|---------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| One-off bootstrap or migration | Role recurs across sessions |
| No stable consciousness text yet | Clear channels + instruments defined |
| No supervision card required | Staff card, rules, and retirement path justified |
