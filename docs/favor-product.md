# What Favor Is — Product Definition

**Status:** Product framing, 2026-08-08.  
**Audience:** Operators, stewards, implementers.  
**Related:** [verb-noun-favors.md](./verb-noun-favors.md) · [favor-barter-solvency.md](./favor-barter-solvency.md) · [microslice-solvency-formula.md](./microslice-solvency-formula.md) · [tech-spec.md](./tech-spec.md)

---

## One sentence

Favor is a **personal social-capital ledger**: a place to lock, track, value, and redeem real-world network leverage so favors stop disappearing into thin air.

---

## What it is not

| Not this | Why |
|----------|-----|
| A lawn-mowing / odd-job marketplace | That framing shrinks the product to commodities anyone can buy with cash |
| State social credit | No government score, no civic ranking, no moral worth of the person |
| A soul score or composite personality rank | SCORE-NAMESPACES law: axes counsel; policy authorizes; no composite |
| Free labor disguised as networking | Work is **pro favor** (por favor) — an explicit trade, not unpaid goodwill |

---

## What it is

People know people. They do things for each other. Most of that value evaporates because there is no net around the butterflies.

Favor puts a net around those opportunities:

1. **Lock** — a verb or noun obligation is recorded with parties, terms, and a timeline.
2. **Track** — delivery is witnessed; the record does not forget who did what for whom.
3. **Value** — community and proof systems price the favor honestly (YONAW + receipts), not by self-claimed sticker prices.
4. **Redeem** — the holder can cash out, trade, or call the locked obligation when it is worth more than money.

The unit of record is a **P-more** credit (Perceived Moment of Real Energy) tied to a concrete favor — not a generic token.

## Core use cases (the product language)

| Use case | Example |
|----------|---------|
| Network access | "I know a chef; they get you a table." |
| Soft power | A pastor's endorsement opens a paid church gig and a restaurant side gig |
| Asymmetric barter | Provider brings a high-value connection that costs them almost nothing; receiver would have paid tens of thousands |
| Business relationship capital | Discounts, tickets, introductions, warehouse space, concierge access |
| Tracking unpaid leverage | Keyboard player does the picnic **pro favor**; the system remembers so later opportunities can be claimed |

Primary surface language: **business relationships, networking, opportunity leverage, access** — not chores.

---

## Verb vs Noun (summary)

Every favor is classified as:

- **Verb favor** — someone **does** something (cook, introduce, play, endorse, appear).
- **Noun favor** — someone **provides** something (warehouse, car, assistant, tickets, table access, a person-connection).

Full taxonomy: [verb-noun-favors.md](./verb-noun-favors.md).

---

## Payment / solvency (summary)

A job can be paid in:

- full cash,
- a mix of cash + accepted favors from the payer's **proposed-favors catalog**,
- or a locked favor obligation instead of cash (with optional cash escrow as the alternative claim).

Values must be **proven**, not invented. Full model: [favor-barter-solvency.md](./favor-barter-solvency.md).

## Measurement stack (already designed)

| Layer | Role |
|-------|------|
| Microslice stencil (V·N·D·Q·T) | Is the intent solvent enough to lock? |
| Moment Matrix | Multi-axis counsel on the utterance / claim |
| YONAW | Community valuation of the completed favor |
| Performance / Credibility / Reach / Y-score | Reputation after delivery |
| IdentityScore + optional ZK | Sybil resistance |

Scores **counsel**. Policy and contracts **authorize**. Witnesses **prove**.

---

## Butterfly-net metaphor (operator language)

Opportunities are butterflies. Most people wave at them and they fly away. Favor is the net: catch the obligation, keep the proof, redeem when the asymmetric value is highest.

---

## Open product questions

1. Legal enforceability of locked favors (honor contract vs court-ready instrument) — jurisdiction and product stance TBD.
2. Default timeline windows for unredeemed obligations.
3. How aggressively the system blocks self-inflated noun values with no proof history.
4. Tax / accounting treatment of favor-as-compensation (out of band for v0 design).
